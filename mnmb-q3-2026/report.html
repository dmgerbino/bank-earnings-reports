Merchants & Marine Bancorp · OTCQX: MNMB · Q3 2026

Quarter ended Sep 30, 2026 (calendar Q3). Released Nov 20, 2026, 10:32 a.m. ET via Business Wire. The holding company's bank, Merchants & Marine Bank, was renamed TownBridge Bank on Sep 28, 2026; it bought Farmers-Merchants Bank (Breaux Bridge, Louisiana) on Apr 1, 2026. All figures GAAP. The release prints only year-to-date income statements, so every quarterly figure here is a year-to-date difference.

Against expectations

No consensus estimate was found. MNMB trades on OTCQX with roughly 600 shares changing hands on an average day, and no analyst estimate turned up in search or earnings-day coverage. The company gives no guidance, so the actual-versus-estimate chart is skipped. Against its own past, net income of $1.46M is up 2.8% from $1.42M a year ago.

EPS here is net income to common divided by period-end common shares (1,359,918 at Sep 30, from common stock at $2.50 par): $1.08 against $1.07. The company's own year-to-date EPS ($9.13 through September, $8.36 through June) implies $0.77 for the quarter; the release does not say what share count it uses, so EPS is not headlined.

What moved

This is the first full quarter with Farmers-Merchants Bank. Net interest income rose 36.7% to $13.24M and interest income 34.1%; loans are 48% higher than a year ago and deposits 59% higher. Deposit cost held at 0.74%, up from 0.43% in Q1, before the acquisition. Revenue rose $4.38M, but operating expense took $4.15M of it, and a higher provision and taxes took most of the rest.

Q2 2026 net income of $11.5M includes acquisition fair-value marks that lifted noninterest income to $23.3M, merger costs in expense, and a $2.6M tax benefit. Past-due ratios before Dec 31, 2025 were not gathered.

Against peers

Against three Gulf South community banks that reported the same calendar quarter, MNMB grew revenue fastest because of the acquisition, but its expenses grew fastest too and its efficiency ratio is the highest. Two of the peers' profit jumps include one-time items, noted below.

    Guidance

    MNMB gives no numeric guidance and there is no consensus, so the guidance chart is skipped. Management expects the past-due loans to be resolved before year-end and the core system conversion in January 2027. The board authorized buying back up to 3% of shares through June 30, 2027.

    Profitability

    Net income rose $0.04M from a year ago. Net interest income added $3.56M and fee income $0.98M; higher operating expense took $4.15M, the provision $0.14M, the securities sale loss $0.16M and taxes $0.05M. Pre-provision net revenue was $2.10M ($2.26M before the securities loss, the figure management cites).
