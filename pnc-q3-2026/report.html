PNC beat on EPS with the lowest charge-offs of six super-regional peers, but it carries the thinnest capital

Quarter ended Sep 30, 2026 (Jul 1 to Sep 30). Released Oct 15, 2026. Figures in USD. Data as of Nov 12, 2026.

The quarter in six numbers

Estimates: Zacks, S&P Global Market Intelligence and Visible Alpha (via StockStory), MarketBeat, all pre-release. NIM and efficiency estimates are Zacks five-analyst averages.

EPS cleared every estimate; revenue and NIM fell just short

Adjusted EPS of $5.09 was 2.2% to 3.7% above the three estimates we found. Total revenue of $6.56B (GAAP) was 0.6% to 0.7% below a $6.60B to $6.61B consensus, and net interest income was about 1% under StockStory's $4.25B estimate.

Management's Q3 guidance from July was not gathered for this run, so actual versus prior guidance is not shown.

Net interest income did the lifting: up 15.3% from a year ago

NII rose to $4.21B and supplied $558M of the $647M year-over-year revenue gain. NIM is 15 bp higher than a year ago but dipped 2 bp from Q2. The year-over-year growth includes FirstBank, acquired Jan 5, 2026 (about $16B of loans). Fee income fell 6% from a strong Q2, when capital markets fees were $577M against $433M this quarter.

Revenue grew $647M year over year, 86% of it from net interest income

Best credit and second-best efficiency of six; last on capital

Against five super-regional competitors' Q3 2026 results (all quarters ended Sep 30, 2026), PNC had the lowest net charge-off ratio and the second-lowest efficiency ratio. Its estimated 9.8% CET1 ratio is the lowest of the group. Panels are sorted by how far PNC sits from the peer median, worst first.

    Q4 outlook: NII up about 4%, but expenses up about 5%

    On the Oct 15 call, CFO Rob Reilly guided Q4 against Q3. Applied to Q3, that implies roughly $4.37B of NII, $2.22B of fees and $3.91B of adjusted expense. Next-quarter EPS consensus is $5.10 (16 analysts, as of Nov 12), essentially flat with Q3's $5.09.

    Other noninterest income guided to about $250M and net charge-offs to about $250M. Full-year 2026 outlook versus 2025: revenue up about 13.7%, NII up about 15.5%, noninterest expense up about 9.5%, effective tax rate about 19.5%. The prior outlook was not gathered, so we cannot say whether the full-year view was raised.

    "Importantly, we continue to demonstrate strong momentum across the franchise." Rob Reilly, CFO, on the call

    Pretax income rose $335M as revenue outran a $290M expense increase

    Noninterest expense fell 8.5% from Q2, which carried $127M of integration costs and a $140M foundation contribution. The efficiency ratio improved to 57% from 59% a year ago, and adjusted expense grew 7.6% against 10.9% revenue growth, about 3.3 points of operating leverage.

    GAAP EPS of $4.97 becomes $5.09 adjusted after $0.12 of integration and Visa items

    Items: FirstBank integration costs of $36M ($27M expense, $9M in noninterest income) and a net $25M Visa-related loss ($11M gain, $36M Class B-3 derivative adjustment), $48M after tax.

    CET1 sits 3.3 points above the well-capitalized minimum, below its 10% target

    The 6.5% well-capitalized threshold is a regulatory minimum, not a target. Management's stated target is 10%. CET1 was 10.7% a year ago, before FirstBank.

    Risks and open questions

    • Capital. CET1 of 9.8% is 0.9 points under the peer median and 0.2 below the 10% target, while PNC lifted its dividend 17.6% to $2.00 and plans Q4 buybacks near Q3's $0.6B.
    • Margin. NIM of 2.94% missed the 3.0% estimate and ranks fifth of six; Q4 NII growth of about 4% depends on loans growing about 2.5%.
    • Reserve release. Provision of $189M ran $68M below $257M of charge-offs, and the reserve ratio fell to 1.42% from 1.61% a year ago.
    • Expense run rate. Guided Q4 adjusted expense up about 5%, faster than the 4% NII growth guided.
    • Fees. Capital markets fees fell $144M from Q2, and total fee income was down 6% sequentially.

    What to watch in Q4

    • NII near $4.37B and NIM moving toward the 3% management says its math points to.
    • CET1 back toward 10% while buybacks continue at about $0.6B.
    • Net charge-offs near the guided $250M and whether provision keeps running below them.
    • Adjusted expense near $3.9B as FirstBank integration costs wind down.
    • Peers report again in mid-January 2027: U.S. Bancorp, Truist, Citizens, Fifth Third and KeyCorp.

    What the data doesn't show

    • CET1 of 9.8% is the release's estimate; the final figure comes with the 10-Q and regulatory filings.
    • Revenue consensus providers don't state GAAP or FTE basis; we compared against GAAP revenue. Zacks shows $6.59B, which looks like an FTE figure.
    • No LSEG or FactSet consensus was found in free coverage.
    • PNC's Q3 guidance from July and its prior full-year outlook were not gathered, so beat or miss against guidance is not shown.
    • PNC's proxy compensation peer group could not be read; peers were chosen as direct competitors.
    • Year-over-year growth for PNC and Fifth Third includes acquisitions, and Truist's quarter carries a securities loss, so growth ranks are not like-for-like.
    • NIM for Q3 2024 to Q2 2025 was rebuilt from the spread plus the noninterest-bearing funds benefit; the method matches the reported Q3 2025 figure.
