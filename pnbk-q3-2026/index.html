PNBK Q3 2026 Earnings

Patriot National Bancorp (PNBK) · Q3 2026 · Jul 1 to Sep 30, 2026 · filed Nov 12, 2026

Net interest income more than doubled, but expenses still ate 94 cents of every revenue dollar

Patriot National Bancorp earned $1.2 million in Q3 2026, against a $2.7 million loss a year earlier, as net interest income rose 114% on a balance sheet that grew 38% this year. Noninterest expense rose 52%, led by a $4.1 million jump in professional fees, leaving an efficiency ratio of 94% against a 56% peer median. The open question is how much of the $3.1 million in other income repeats.

Profitability

Higher expenses absorbed 57% of the gain in income

Pretax income, Q3 2025 to Q3 2026, $ thousands. Noninterest expense rose $5.4M, of which professional and outside services rose $4.1M and salaries $1.3M. The company reports GAAP only; there is no adjusted figure to reconcile.

Risks and open questions

  • Professional fees were $4.8M vs $0.7M a year ago. A secondary summary ties this to SVB-related legal costs net of insurance recoveries; the filing's MD&A was not read.
  • Other income of $3.1M (vs $0.1M) made up 18.5% of revenue. If it does not repeat, Q3 would have been a pretax loss.
  • Reserve coverage thinned: allowance fell to 0.94% of loans from 1.15% at year end while loans grew 52%. A 2026 accounting change records purchased-loan allowances outside provision.
  • Non-accrual loans of about $25.4M, 2.8% of loans (secondary source).
  • Equity fell to $83.2M from $94.7M at year end; unrealized securities losses widened to $33.9M.
  • A secondary summary reports a Nasdaq minimum bid price notice on Nov 10, 2026.

What to watch next quarter

  • Other income: does the $3.1M hold, or fall back toward the $0.1M run rate?
  • Professional and outside services: back under $1M would cut the efficiency ratio by about 23 points.
  • Net interest income above $10.7M as the $1.37B deposit base, 25% noninterest-bearing, is put to work.
  • Allowance to loans and non-accruals as the new loans season.
  • Carver Bancorp (CARV), a similar-size New York bank, reports its September quarter next.
