Quarter ended Sep 30, 2026 (calendar Q3). The 10-Q was filed Nov 13, 2026; the release came out Nov 17, 2026, 4:15 p.m. ET via GlobeNewswire. All figures GAAP; the company reports no adjusted EPS. Basic and diluted EPS are the same. Return on assets, return on equity and the margin in the release are for Embassy Bank, not the holding company.

Against expectations

No consensus estimate was found. Embassy trades on OTCQX and no analyst publishes estimates for it; Benzinga and TipRanks list the stock with no Q3 2026 forecast. The company gives no guidance, so the actual-versus-estimate chart is skipped. Against its own past, EPS of $0.66 is up 32.0% from $0.50 and 17.9% from Q2's $0.56.

What moved

Net interest income rose 16.9% from a year ago. Interest income rose $1.36M while interest expense fell $0.47M, as the cost of funds dropped to 1.64% from 1.81%. The bank-level margin widened 32 bp to 2.84%. Noninterest expense rose 6.7%, slower than revenue (+15.0%), so the computed efficiency ratio improved 4.3 points. Q4 2025 and Q1 2026 are left out of the trend lines because the quarterly detail for them was not gathered.

Against peers

Against two Pennsylvania community banks quoted on OTCQX that reported the same calendar quarter, Embassy grew net income fastest but has the lowest margin and return on assets. Its growth starts from a lower base of profitability, and ENB's growth includes a February acquisition.

* EMYB return on assets and margin are bank-level figures from the release (the consolidated tax-equivalent margin in the 10-Q was 2.89%). CCFN's margin is tax-equivalent; ENBP's basis is not stated. Efficiency for all three is noninterest expense divided by net interest income plus noninterest income, computed here (ENBP reports 68.8%).

    Guidance

    Embassy gives no numeric guidance and there is no consensus, so the guidance chart is skipped. The board raised the annual dividend to $0.55 a share from $0.48.

    Profitability

    Net income rose $1.06M from a year ago. Net interest income added $1.83M and the provision benefit $0.10M; higher expenses took $0.48M, lower fee income $0.07M and taxes $0.32M.

    Net interest income did nearly all the work

    Bank capital ratios sit well above well-capitalized minimums

    Thresholds are the regulatory well-capitalized minimums for a bank, not targets. CET1 was 14.4% and leverage 9.1% at Dec 31, 2025.

    GAAP and adjusted

    The company reports GAAP only, so there is nothing to reconcile. No one-time items were called out for the quarter.

    Risks and open questions

      What to watch next quarter

      • Cost of funds (1.64%): the margin gain rests on it; watch whether it keeps falling.
      • Net interest margin (2.84% bank level): a fourth quarter of widening would narrow the gap to peers.
      • Accumulated other comprehensive loss ($52.4M): moves with bond prices and drives book value.
      • Loan growth (net loans +4.7% this year, loans 82% of deposits).
      • Peers CCFN and ENBP report Q4 2026 in late January 2027.
