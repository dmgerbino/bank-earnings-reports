Quarter ended Sep 30, 2026 (calendar Q3). Released Nov 12, 2026, 4:10 p.m. ET. All figures GAAP; OptimumBank reports no adjusted EPS.

Against expectations

Both lines beat the Zacks consensus by about 11%. Without the roughly $0.9 million late fee collected on a nonaccrual loan payoff, revenue still beat by 6%. The company gives no quarterly guidance, so there is no guidance range to measure against.

EPS and revenue both cleared the Zacks estimate by 11%

What moved

Revenue and net interest income rose in each of the last five quarters. Margin widened as loan yields (7.40%) rose faster than funding costs (3.49% on interest-bearing liabilities).

Against peers

OPHC ranks first of four on revenue growth, net interest margin, efficiency and return on assets. It is second on net charge-offs and Tier 1 leverage. All peers reported the same calendar quarter (Jul 1 to Sep 30, 2026).

    Guidance

    OptimumBank did not give numeric guidance. The CEO said management has found further ways to "improve operating efficiency and limit expenses," with benefits expected over the next several quarters. No next-quarter consensus was found to compare against.

    Profitability

    Net income rose $2.85 million from a year ago. Higher net interest income added $5.73 million, more than double the $2.39 million rise in operating expense.

    Net interest income drove the whole $2.85M year-over-year gain

    GAAP and adjusted

    There is no adjusted figure to reconcile. One item to keep in mind: about $0.9 million of Q3 noninterest income came from late fees on a nonaccrual loan payoff. Excluding it, noninterest income was about $1.94 million, 2.3% below a year ago, and net income would be roughly $0.7 million lower after tax at the quarter's 24.9% effective rate (an estimate).

    Risks and open questions

      What to watch next quarter

      • Reserve to loans (0.89%): does it hold or keep thinning as loans grow?
      • Cost of interest-bearing liabilities (3.49%, up 19 bp QoQ): a further rise would squeeze the 4.63% margin.
      • Noninterest expense ($9.0M): the test of the promised efficiency program.
      • Noninterest income without one-time fees (about $1.94M this quarter).
      • Diluted share count after ATM and private share sales.
      • Peers USCB, CCBG and BAFN report Q4 2026 in late January 2027.

      What the data doesn't show

      • Only one consensus provider (Zacks) was found; its preview printed revenue as "$17.65 billion," which its own +35.5% growth figure shows is $17.65 million. An Investing.com story headlined an "earnings miss" could not be read, so its estimate is not shown.
      • CET1 and total risk-based capital ratios, deposit cost and weighted share counts were not in the release text read; the 10-Q was not yet checked.
      • EPS history uses the company's restated diluted figures after the April 2026 exchange of preferred stock for 11.46 million nonvoting common shares; basic and diluted EPS were not comparable before Q3.
      • Income statement credit loss expense ($1,066K) and the allowance rollforward ($1,111K) differ; the release does not explain the gap.
      • Peer beat or miss against their own consensus was not gathered.

      Method and sources

      OptimumBank figures are GAAP from the Q3 2026 earnings release (GlobeNewswire, Nov 12, 2026). Revenue is net interest income plus noninterest income. Growth rates and margins were recomputed from dollar amounts. The net charge-off ratio for OPHC is $11K annualized over average loans. Consensus is pre-release, from a Zacks earnings preview; the EPS estimate was unchanged over the prior seven days, and the analyst count was not given. Peer data is each bank's Q3 2026 8-K Exhibit 99.1. The BAFN efficiency ratio is computed from its release, and BAFN capital ratios are bank-level. The 5% Tier 1 leverage line is the regulatory well-capitalized minimum, not a target. Data was read on Nov 13, 2026.
