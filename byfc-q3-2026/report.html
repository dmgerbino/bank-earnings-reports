Broadway Financial Q3 2026
Broadway Financial · Nasdaq: BYFC · Q3 2026

Quarter ended Sep 30, 2026 (calendar Q3). Released Nov 13, 2026, 4:05 p.m. ET. GAAP unless noted; EPS is net income to common after preferred dividends.

Against expectations

No beat or miss can be stated. No pre-release consensus was found (MarketBeat, Chartmill and Seeking Alpha showed no usable estimate, and no earnings-day wire story named one), and Broadway gives no guidance. The bullet chart is skipped for that reason.

What moved

Net interest income rose in every quarter shown, funded by deposits that grew $260.8M in a year. The margin did not widen: loan yield rose 4 bp from Q2 to 5.26% while the cost of interest-bearing liabilities rose 3 bp to 3.05%. Q3 2025 is distorted by a $25.9M goodwill impairment, so the trend charts avoid net income.

Against peers

Against three community banks of similar size that reported the same calendar quarter, Broadway grew net interest income slowest, had the lowest verified margin and the second-highest cost ratio. Its capital is mid-pack, but its CBLR is a different measure from peers' Tier 1 leverage.

n/v = not verified. *Bank-level, secondary source. Leverage ratios are not identical measures.

    Guidance

    Broadway gives no numeric guidance and no next-quarter consensus was found. CEO Brian Argrett said the focus is on "optimizing the balance sheet, enhancing profitability." The chart is skipped.

    Profitability

    Net income to common rose $174K from Q2. Lower provision (+$813K) and higher net interest income (+$406K) outweighed an $855K rise in expenses and a $454K drop in fee income (Q2 included a one-time $450K New Markets Tax Credit fee). The bridge runs from Q2 because Q3 2025 includes the goodwill impairment.

    A lower provision did most of the work in the quarter-over-quarter gain

    GAAP and non-GAAP

    The company also reports net income before preferred dividends: $1.1M ($0.13 a share) in Q3, against $968K in Q2. The gap to GAAP net income to common is the preferred dividend. "Taxes and preferred" in the bridge is derived as the residual.

    Risks and open questions

      What to watch next quarter

      • Net interest margin (2.64%): growth needs to come with a wider spread, not just a bigger balance sheet.
      • Noninterest expense ($8.3M): a fall back toward $7.5M would put efficiency near 72%.
      • Nonaccrual loans (1.20% of loans) and the 0.87% reserve.
      • Book value ($11.37) and the CBLR (12.83%) if rates keep rising.
      • Carver Bancorp (CARV), another minority depository institution, reports its September quarter next.
